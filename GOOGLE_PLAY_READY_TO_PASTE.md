## 6. Privacy Policy
**Location**: Store Listing → Privacy policy

You have two options:

### Option A: Quick Privacy Policy (Recommended)
Go to: https://www.privacypolicygenerator.info/
- Select "App" as type
- Fill in your app details
- Generate policy
- Copy the URL and paste in Google Play Console

### Option B: Simple Privacy Policy Text
```
PRIVACY POLICY

Challenge Education is committed to protecting your privacy.
```

## 9. Screenshots & Media

### Screenshots Required:
Minimum 2, recommended 5-8

**Dimensions**: 1080x1920 pixels (or 9:16 aspect ratio)

**What to include:**
1. **Home Screen** - Main dashboard
2. **Challenge List** - Available challenges
3. **Challenge Creation** - Creating new challenges
4. **Real-time Scoring** - Live results screen
5. **Rankings/Leaderboard** - Team/student rankings
6. **Progress Report** - Statistics and analytics

### App Icon:
- **Size**: 512x512 pixels (PNG)
- **Must be**: Your app's icon/logo
- **No text** on the icon itself
