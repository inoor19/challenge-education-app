## 15. AFTER SUBMISSION

**What Google does:**
1. Reviews your app (1-24 hours, usually 2-4 hours)
2. Checks for:
   - Functionality (app works)
   - Security (no malware)
   - Content (matches description)
   - Policy compliance (follows rules)

**If approved:**
- App shows in Play Store search results
- Users can download and install
- App is live! 🎉

**If rejected:**
- Email explains why
- Fix the issue
- Increment version code (+1)
- Rebuild and re-submit
- Usually approved on second try

- versionName (1.0.0): User-visible version
